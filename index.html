Honest by design

Private. Clear about its limits.

What KYH is

  • A preparation tool to help you ask better questions.
  • Fully in-browser. No data leaves your device, nothing is stored on a server.
  • Optional session memory that you can clear anytime.
  • Built to work alongside a qualified healthcare professional.

What it is not

  • Not medical advice or a diagnosis.
  • Not for special-care situations such as pregnancy, recent surgery or hospitalisation, or food allergies.
  • Not a replacement for a doctor's visit. Please consult one before acting on anything here.
