Build your health profile,
in three dimensions.

Your Health Profile walks you through three connected dimensions of everyday health — what to test for, how to spend your 24 hours, and what to eat — and builds each recommendation around the profile you give it. Nothing you enter leaves your browser.

01 · Diagnostics

Establish a baseline

We map your age, sex, and family history to a risk category, then narrow a 100+ test library down to the ones actually relevant to you — so screening is targeted, not guesswork.

02 · Daily Routine

Structure the day

We compare how you currently spend your 24 hours — sleep, desk time, movement — against an ideal built for your profile, and flag where the gap matters most.

03 · Diet

Fuel with precision

Your sex, age, body composition, and the activity level we see in your routine translate into calorie, macro, and micronutrient targets — adjusted for how you actually eat, from whole foods to packaged snacks.
