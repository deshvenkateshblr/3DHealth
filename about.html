Before you begin

A quick, honest picture of what KYH does — and what it does not — so you can decide if this tool is right for you right now.

What it is

Your health, in three dimensions

KYH helps you understand your own health across Diet, Daily Routine, and Diagnostics.

It organizes your details in a clear, insightful way so you can take more informed decisions — always in partnership with a qualified healthcare professional.

You can expand your learning with AI prompts and curated videos from popular, reputed sources.

Everything stays private: no data leaves your browser, and we store nothing about you on any server.

What it is not

Not advice. Not for special care.

It is not health advice of any kind. All recommendations are exploratory and must not be taken literally without explicit consultation with a healthcare professional.

It is not intended for special cases, including:

  • Pregnancy
  • Recent surgery
  • Recent hospitalization
  • Allergies requiring specialized management
  • Any other special-care scenario

If any of these apply, please work with your care team first. You can return when your situation is stable.
