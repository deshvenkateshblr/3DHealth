What it is

Your health, in three dimensions

KYH helps you understand your own health across Diet, Daily Routine, and Diagnostics.

It organizes your details in a clear, insightful way so you can take more informed decisions in partnership with a qualified healthcare professional.

You can expand your learning with AI prompts and curated videos from popular, reputed sources. Everything stays private: no data leaves your browser, and we store nothing about you on any server. more…

What it is not

Not advice. Not for special care.

It is not health advice of any kind. All recommendations are exploratory and must not be taken literally without explicit consultation with a healthcare professional.

It is not intended for special cases, including Pregnancy, Recent surgery or hospitalization, Food Allergies or ny other special-care scenarios

If any of these apply, please work with your care team first. You can return when your situation is stable. more…

Medical Sources

Peer-reviewed and curated data

To ensure the logic is medically sound, KYH relies on peer-reviewed and globally recognized medical databases.

The engine's logic is validated against the NIH Office of Dietary Supplements for verified deficiency symptoms, The Merck Manual and Linus Pauling Institute for nutritional research, ACSM & APTA for movement guidelines, and Mayo Clinic for foundational lifestyle protocols. This evidence base helps you bring structured, clinically grounded questions to your healthcare provider. more…
